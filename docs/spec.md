# Pawsome Mobile Grooming: the booking app

Pawsome Mobile Grooming is a made-up business used in the Agentic Builders course. Replace its details with yours
whenever you like.

## The business

- A mobile dog groomer: a van that comes to the customer's home.
- Phone: (555) 010-4242. Service area: within 15 miles of Millbrook.
- Working hours: Tuesday to Saturday, 8 am to 5 pm. Closed Sunday and Monday.

## Services and prices

| Service | Small dog (under 25 lb) | Medium (25–60 lb) | Large (over 60 lb) | Takes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bath and brush | $55 | $70 | $85 | 1 hour |
| Full groom (bath, haircut, nails) | $75 | $95 | $120 | 2 hours |
| Nail trim | $20 | $20 | $25 | 30 minutes |

## What customers can do

1. See the services and prices.
2. Pick an open time slot.
3. Give their name, address, phone, and their dog's name and size.
4. Create an account and see or cancel their own bookings.
5. Pay a deposit by card ($20), and get a confirmation email and a reminder the day before.

## What the owner can do

1. Sign in.
2. Set services, prices and working hours.
3. See the day's bookings in order, with addresses.
4. Mark a job done.
5. Refund a cancelled deposit.

## Rules

- No double-booking: a time slot can be booked once.
- A slot is held while the customer pays.
- Customers can cancel up to 24 hours before for a full refund of the deposit (the owner can change this).

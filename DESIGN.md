# How the app looks

A friendly, plain look for a local business. Change it to suit yours: ask your AI helper to update this file first,
then the pages.

## Colors

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| paper | `#fffaf2` | page background |
| card | `#ffffff` | boxes on the page |
| line | `#e6dccb` | borders and dividers |
| ink | `#1d2b2a` | text |
| muted | `#566260` | small print, hints |
| accent | `#0f7b6c` | the one accent: buttons, links, the selected item |

One accent color only. No gradients, glows or blurred backgrounds.

## Type

- Headings: **Bricolage Grotesque** (Google Fonts), bold.
- Body: **Source Sans 3** (Google Fonts), 18px, line height 1.6.
- Load both with `next/font/google`.

## Shapes and spacing

- Corners: 6px. Borders: 1px `line`. Shadows: none, or a flat 3px offset in `line`.
- Buttons: accent background, white text, at least 44px tall.
- Text left-aligned. Pages use the full width on a computer with comfortable side margins; one column on a phone.

## Words

Plain and friendly, as the owner would say it on the phone. Prices with the dollar sign and no cents ("$55").
